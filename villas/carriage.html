Text About CarriageAll Villas

Black modern architecture with a games room and a pool under the oaks.

A striking black angular house set on a lawn of old oaks, with a lit pool and loungers out front and a covered wood-ceiling patio with an outdoor kitchen. Inside is dark and playful — royal-portrait art, a billiards table, shuffleboard, a black stone kitchen with bar seating and a wine fridge. Bedrooms include kings, a blush suite and two bunk rooms, so a big crew fits without doubling up.
