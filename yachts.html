The Water

Biscayne Bay by day, the skyline by night — captained, stocked, sized to your group.

Four-hour charters are the standard; sunset and midnight slots book fastest. Most boats carry 13 guests under Coast Guard rules; we have 30- to 50-guest yachts for the big nights. Captain and crew included, pickup from Brickell and Miami Beach marinas, jet skis by the hour to match. We've run these boats for our own parties — July 4th link-ups, birthdays, three-yacht fireworks nights.
